# How LLMs Work (in my own words)

Large Language Models (LLMs) are advanced AI models trained on massive amounts of text data. They function fundamentally by predicting the next most likely word (or "token") in a sequence. Under the hood, they use a neural network architecture called a Transformer, which allows the model to understand the context and relationships between words in a sentence, no matter how far apart they are.

### Basic Architecture Diagram

```text
[User Prompt]
      ↓
[Tokenization (converting words to numbers)]
      ↓
[Transformer Layers (Attention Mechanism checks word relationships)]
      ↓
[Next Token Prediction (Guessing the best next word)]
      ↓
[Generated Text Output]
```
